Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

In short:
  • All purchases are final — we do not give refunds for credits or plans.
  • Paid but your credits didn't arrive? We add them to your account.
  • If the AI fails to create your document, your credit comes back automatically.

This policy covers the Starter (R30) and Pro (R99) plans, which are once-off payments made through PayFast. Please make sure the plan is right for you before you pay. You can try ResumeAI SA first with the free credit you get when you sign up.

1. No refunds

All payments are final. Because your credits are added to your account straight after payment and can be used immediately, we do not refund plans or credits, whether they are used or unused. Credits never expire, so you can use them whenever you need them.

2. If something goes wrong, we fix it with credits

  • Payment taken but no credits: credits are usually added within a minute. If they have not arrived after an hour, email us and we will add the credits you paid for to your account.
  • The AI failed to create your document: the credit is returned to your account automatically. If it was not, tell us and we will add it back.
  • Any other technical problem: contact us and, where the problem was on our side, we will add replacement credits to your account.

3. Charged twice by mistake

If you were charged more than once for the same purchase, we will refund the extra payment through PayFast. This is the only case in which money is paid back.

4. How to report a problem

Email support@resumeaisa.co.za from the email address on your ResumeAI SA account, with:

  • the date you paid and the amount;
  • the PayFast payment reference from your receipt, if you have it;
  • a short note about the problem.

We aim to reply within 2 working days.

5. Free credit

The free credit you get when you sign up has no cash value.

6. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy takes away any rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.

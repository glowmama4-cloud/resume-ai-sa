Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

In short:
  • We only use your information to build your CV and run your account.
  • We never sell your information.
  • Your card details go to PayFast, never to us.
  • Your Z83 answers stay on your own phone or computer.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

This policy explains how ResumeAI SA (“we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information when you use resumeaisa.co.za. We follow the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (the password is stored securely by our login provider; we cannot see it).
  • CV information: what you type or upload to build your CV — for example your work history, education, skills, languages and contact details — and the CVs, cover letters and LinkedIn bios we create for you.
  • Job search information: jobs you save, and any applications, statuses, notes and follow-up dates you record in “My Job Search”.
  • Payment records: the plan you bought, the amount, the date and the PayFast payment reference. Your card or bank details are entered on PayFast's website and are never sent to us.
  • Credits: how many CV credits your account has.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • Z83 form: your Z83 answers are filled into the PDF inside your own browser. They are not sent to us or stored by us. If you tick “Remember my details”, they are saved only on your own device, and you can untick it to remove them.
  • We do not use advertising trackers on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and save your CVs, cover letters and LinkedIn bios.
  • To run your account, keep you signed in, and keep track of your credits and payments.
  • To show you jobs and let you save and track applications.
  • To help you when you contact us, and to prevent abuse of the service.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Service providers we use

We share information only with the companies that help us run the service, and only what they need:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): the details you give us to write your CV, cover letter or LinkedIn bio are sent to Anthropic's AI to generate the text.
  • Supabase: stores your account, CVs, saved jobs and credits.
  • Vercel: hosts our website.
  • PayFast: processes payments.

Some of these providers store information on servers outside South Africa. We only use providers that protect personal information with security measures appropriate to the information they handle.

5. Job adverts and applications

Job listings come from public sources such as the DPSA public service vacancy circulars and employers' own adverts. When you click “Apply”, you go to the employer's own website or instructions — we do not submit applications or send your CV to employers.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your account and saved CVs for as long as your account is open, so you can come back to them. Payment records are kept for as long as the law requires for financial records. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account, CVs and job search information, except records we must keep by law.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information or your whole account;
  • stop using your information for a particular purpose.

Email us at support@resumeaisa.co.za from the email address on your account. If you are not happy with how we handled your request, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

8. Security

Your information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), your account is protected by your password, and access to your information is limited to what is needed to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it.

9. Young job seekers

If you are under 18, please use ResumeAI SA with the permission of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
